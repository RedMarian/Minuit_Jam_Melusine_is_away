# How to Play: Melusine Is Away

Melusine is taking her bath, and her secret is her serpent tail. Her husband (and a few other curious people) keep sneaking up to peek at her. Keep her tail hidden for **one minute**. The bar at the top fills up as the minute goes by.

## Controls

Press two arrow keys together, one up/down and one left/right, to make Melusine lean that way. Lean **away** from the peeker so her body hides her tail.

| Peeker comes from  | Press        |
| ------------------ | ------------ |
| Top left (window)  | Down + Right |
| Top right (window) | Down + Left  |
| Bottom left        | Up + Right   |
| Bottom right       | Up + Left    |

You don't need to hold the keys. Melusine keeps her last pose until you press a new pair.

## Peekers and warnings

- Peekers at the top pop up from behind the window.
- Peekers at the bottom walk in from off screen, so a red **!!!** flashes in that corner first.
- A peeker only looks for a split second. You get a short grace window, so pressing a little late still counts.

## The last 15 seconds

After 45 seconds the peekers start playing tricks: a **!!!** may flash in a bottom corner while someone pops up from a different direction. Watch where they actually appear before you commit.
